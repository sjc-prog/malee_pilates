Booking & studio policy

Book group classes at least 12 hours ahead. Cancel or reschedule at least 12 hours before a group class, or 24 hours before a private session, to keep your pass within its validity. Late cancellations and no-shows use the pass. Packages and single sessions are non-refundable in cash.

Group classes with no bookings two hours before their start are cancelled automatically. Arrive 10–15 minutes early, wear grip socks for Reformer, and tell your instructor about injuries, medical conditions or pregnancy. The studio is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged belongings.

For private sessions with additional participants, please contact the studio before booking so the team can arrange the Reformer spaces and the ฿1,000 per-person supplement.
